# Known issues (found during the refactor, not changed)

These pre-date the refactor. Behaviour was preserved on purpose; each needs a product decision.

0. **Consolidation notes (newest).**
   * **RESOLVED (Phase B.1 review, applied in the consolidation): approved area compared without units.** "1 Acre", "500 Sq. Meters" or "600 Sq. Yards" against a record in Sq. Ft. could end `verified`. Areas are now compared in square yards; marla/kanal/gaz and unit-less numbers are *unknown* (-> unable to verify). Still open: V3 (POA can end `verified` with an unconfirmed attorney), V4 (`suspicious` is a fourth status), V7 (free search can pick the wrong record).
   * **RESOLVED (G6.1c): `tests/browser/camera_upload_test.py` skipped its upload half.** It used to enter as a guest (document and evidence views are account-only, so it timed out) and, once past that, printed `file inputs present: 0` and silently skipped `set_input_files`. It now enters as a registered account (`enter_as_user`), still checks that a guest gets the login dialog and no evidence buttons, tests camera capture (live fake camera, capture, use photo) and runs four actual upload cases through the real `input#docfile` after choosing a document type: an unsupported `.txt` (refused), a 1x1 PNG (refused as unreadable), a readable PNG and a PDF. A missing upload control or an expected message now fails the test. **Still unverified:** the Tesseract.js and PDF.js CDN requests (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`) are deliberately blocked in this test, so it only proves the graceful failures ("The OCR engine could not be loaded", "PDF reader could not be loaded"). It does not show that OCR or PDF text reading works; that needs the libraries and real documents (see the "Not verifiable in the sandbox" item below).
   * Not verifiable in the sandbox: PostgreSQL, TOTP (`pyotp`), live Google/Resend/AI, real OCR/PDF on real scans, dependency scans, production routing of `/` on Vercel.

1. **PARTLY RESOLVED (Phase B): verification false positives.** Original problem: on the recorded fixtures the free-search pipeline reported *Verified* for `power_of_attorney_mismatch` (OCR and PDF) and `altered_multiple_mismatch` (PDF).
   * **Root causes, all in `frontend/js/domain/extraction.js` `decide()` / `compareToProperty()`:** (a) only the profile's own fields were compared, so a second party (the attorney) and the descriptors a document type does not require (survey, address) were never checked against the record, which let a document with owner/CNIC/plot swapped to another record pass; (b) plot and survey were compared by plain substring, so a truncated or prefix value ("4-C", "34") counted as matching "34-C"/"134-C"; (c) a required field that was found but could not be compared (no CNIC on the record, unreadable approved area) was counted as support, and an unreadable approved-area number was treated as a match.
   * **Fixed:** the attorney's CNIC is cross-checked against the records (a CNIC on record under a different name is a mismatch; a CNIC on no record cannot be contradicted, so the attorney stays declared unchecked); survey/plot/address that a document states must agree with the matched record even when the type does not require them (under OCR this is "unable to verify", never a proven mismatch); plot and survey match on whole tokens; `verified` now requires every required field to be found AND positively compared. Result names are unchanged: `verified`, `not_verified`, `unable_to_verify`, `suspicious`.
   * **Result on the 57 synthetic fixture cases:** exactly three changed, all from `verified` to `not_verified` (`pdf/power_of_attorney_mismatch`, `ocr/power_of_attorney_mismatch`, `pdf/altered_multiple_mismatch`); nothing that was verified before was lost. Baseline is now OCR 17/29, PDF 28/28, **zero false positives**, locked in `tests/frontend/pipeline.test.js`; rule-level tests are in `tests/frontend/verificationRules.test.js`.
   * **Still open / uncertain:** OCR still fails to confirm five valid documents (`sale_deed`, `allotment`, `transfer_order`, `power_of_attorney` matching, `ocr_challenge`); they come out as "unable to verify", never as a mismatch. These results come from 29 invented documents and say nothing about real scans: real-document validation is still needed. A document can still be verified when its attorney (or another extra party) has a CNIC that is on no record. A stricter "verified" can turn a document that was verified against a record lacking a required field (for example a utility bill against the listed properties, which have no address on file) into "unable to verify". Plot/survey/address of the matched record that is only a placeholder ("—") still reads as a mismatch. Area units (sq ft vs sq yards) are not converted. Verification runs in the browser, so it is advisory, not enforcement. Treat results as indicative, never as proof.
